# Public IT CV source review — 2026-09-27

Purpose: identify external material for **engineering rehearsal** while waiting for consented applications to the university's actual IT vacancy. Public CVs are not that vacancy's applicant population and cannot satisfy G5 real shadow or independent HR labeling. Do not commit or redistribute downloaded personal CVs in this repository.

| Source | Provenance/use finding | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| [Hiring-Bias repository](https://github.com/re-cinq/hiring-bias) and [data license](https://github.com/re-cinq/hiring-bias/blob/main/DATA-LICENSE) | Author states the baseline résumé is his own; generated variants contain no third-party personal information. Data are CC BY 4.0. One software-engineering baseline can help exercise parsing and counterfactual fairness checks with attribution. | Suitable for a **small external engineering fixture** after local redaction and attribution. One résumé cannot provide a 30-family holdout or university shadow. |
| [LLMHiringFairnessSG](https://github.com/Social-AI-Studio/LLMHiringFairnessSG) | Project states 100 JDs, 100 neutral baselines and 4,000 demographic variants, CC BY 4.0. It is designed for bias experiments, not real university applications. | Candidate for a separate fairness stress test, provided its schema and attribution are reviewed. Never count variants as independent people. |
| [resume_corpus](https://github.com/florex/resume_corpus) | Includes occupation-labeled résumé text and IT skill terms, but the public repository page does not establish a license or individual consent for these personal documents. | Do not ingest or send to DeepSeek until rights and privacy provenance are verified. |
| [Kaggle Resume Dataset](https://www.kaggle.com/search?q=resume+in%3Adatasets) | Its description says examples were taken from LiveCareer. A public Kaggle listing alone does not establish author permission to process personal résumé content for this project. | Do not treat as consented applicant data or G5 evidence. |

For the actual gate, the school must provide applications to the approved IT JD with documented permitted use. HR and an IT reviewer should record independent rubric labels **before** AI results are shown, then adjudicate disagreements and sign the dated gate record. The assistant's two-role simulation remains a diagnostic rehearsal only.
